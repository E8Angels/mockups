Tagline
AI powered control and sensing that reduces apartment buildings' utility costs 25% in the first hour of use.
Problem
Apartment building owners are facing increasing pain: - Electricity costs are rising 25-35% annually, with volatile peak demand charges - Fewer staff are managing larger portfolios as experienced operators retire - Insurance renewals in jeopardy due to unmanaged freeze, flood, and mold risks
Solution
Embue reduces energy costs immediately, provides building data and controls on mobile, saves site teams time, and supports a zero-down, fast-install product for all-electric heat pump buildings.
Business Model
Portfolio-based sales to owners with direct exposure to energy costs. Revenue includes one-time per-unit revenue, multi-year subscriptions, and Embue Connect contracts for central heat pump buildings.
Market
TAM: 48M US rental units and 16B sqft of medium commercial buildings. SAM: $2.4B ARR across 27M large US multifamily units. SOM: $400M across 5M units where owners pay some or all utilities.
