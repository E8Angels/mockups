Problem
Class 3 commercial fleets electrifying their last-mile routes face 12–18 month lead times on ground-up EV trucks. Existing electric retrofits are bespoke and rely on Tier-2 integrators.
Solution
Drop-in powertrain conversion kit, three SKUs covering 90% of the Class 3 ICE fleet. Installs in <8 hours by a certified shop.
Business model
Hardware unit economics: $18K margin per retrofit at $30K avg price; certified-installer network earns $6K each.
Market
14.2M Class 3 trucks in NA fleets. ~1.7M turning over to electric drivetrains 2026–2030 per ICCT.
Go to market
Land-and-expand with named fleet partners: Sysco regional, FedEx Ground regional, US Foods. Certified-installer expansion in PNW + CA in year 1.
Competitive advantage
Drop-in form factor specific to Class 3 frame; certified-installer playbook; signed pilots in three fleet types.
Traction
137 paid retrofits. $4.2M ARR at $30K avg ticket. 3 fleet customers signed for 2026 (Sysco, FedEx Ground regional, US Foods).
Competitors
Lightning eMotors (ground-up), Motiv, Workhorse — none do drop-in retrofits at this price.
Future milestones
Q3 2026: complete SKU-3. Q4 2026: 20 certified-installer locations. Q2 2027: launch Class 2 SKU.
Patentable ideas
Modular battery-skateboard mounting bracket; software-controlled regen tuning by SKU; pending provisional filings.
Environmental impact
Each retrofit avoids ~18 tons CO2/year. Cumulative avoided emissions at 2026 plan: ~24K tons.
