Instead, every column can have its own summary chosen from the picker at that column's footer cell. Look at the table footer: Capital is set to sum, Last touch is set to latest, and the other columns show ＋ Summary as a dashed button — click any to open the aggregation picker for that column. The Capital picker is open by default in this mockup. Multi-level grouping is supported via the Group-by panel above (currently: Status → Sector).
