Beyond Silicon

Diligence report · July 2026 · Draft

Executive Summary

Beyond Silicon is developing perovskite-silicon tandem solar cells intended to increase module efficiency while fitting into established photovoltaic manufacturing processes.

Product

Technology and value proposition

The company adds a solution-processed perovskite layer to conventional silicon cells. Management expects the architecture to increase panel efficiency from roughly 22% to more than 28%, creating a differentiated premium product for domestic module manufacturers.

The approach is designed as a drop-in extension to existing cell manufacturing. The team has received a U.S. patent covering its tandem device architecture and is developing additional process and material intellectual property.

Technical validation

The current development plan targets full-size cells above 25% efficiency, followed by IEC 61215 testing and customer pilots. Durability, repeatability at scale, and manufacturing yield remain the central technical diligence questions.

Commercialization

Initial market entry is expected through applications that value high efficiency and can tolerate shorter product lifetimes. The company reports letters of intent spanning a niche one-megawatt opportunity and a larger residential-module opportunity.

How the experience fits together

The report has one home. The portal supplies data and coordination; Google Docs supplies the authoring surface.

1

Begin from a section

Each portal section has an Edit report entry point. The first use creates the Doc with current team and deal terms.

2

Land in the right place

The portal launch token opens the requested bookmark and scopes the assistant to that section.

3

Edit natively

Collaborators use normal Google Docs formatting, comments, suggestions, and version history.

4

Draft, then improve

Whole-section drafting or revision comes first; selection-level improvement follows when text is selected.

5

Ask and investigate

Ask E8 answers questions with citations, while toolbar links open findings and evidence in the portal.

6

Stay in context

Cursor movement prompts before switching sections. Non-owners can proceed after a concise warning.

Design principles

  • Google Docs is canonical; the portal does not maintain a competing editable copy.
  • AI proposes before it writes.
  • Fixed template headings eliminate generated-heading translation problems.
  • Portal launch context is primary; cursor polling is a guardrail.
  • Section ownership guides users without blocking collaboration.
  • People without the add-on can still collaborate and use portal whole-section drafting.
