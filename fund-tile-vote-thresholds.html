Fund tile — vote visuals that show the thresholds

Key reframe: the stacked bar is a cumulative progress bar. Segments are ordered largest-check → smallest so each tier's share adds into the tiers below it. Two thresholds matter: 51% to fund (at the highest check size where the cumulative share crosses it) and 67% to pass at all.

Data used on this page: 6 No (30%), 8 $125K (40%), 4 $175K (20%), 2 $225K (10%) — 20 votes of 40 cast.
Cumulative totals: $225K = 10% · $225K + $175K = 30% · $225K + $175K + $125K = 70% (clears 67%, so it passes; at $125K).

Option B · literal

Three cumulative "rung" bars, one per check size

Make the cumulative math visible: three bars, one per candidate outcome ($225K / $175K / $125K). Each bar shows the share of votes that would support that tier or higher. The 51% and 67% threshold lines sit on every track so you can see at a glance which tier passes. Removes all guesswork.
