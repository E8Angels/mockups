Amanda White
Mar 24, 2026
Thanks to everyone who joined us for our Impact Investing Learning Lab cohosted with Bridgespan Social Impact! We covered the spectrum from finance-first to impact-first investing, explored the current climate investing landscape, and discussed how to evaluate the impact of investments.

One of the strengths of our community is that, as individual investors, where you fall on that spectrum is shaped by your own investment thesis. While many large climate funds sit at specific points along this range, each of you has the flexibility to define your own approach and decide where you want to invest.

The session generated excellent discussion about measurement frameworks, the difference between intentionality and additionality, and practical tools for assessing whether your investments are truly creating impact beyond financial returns.
