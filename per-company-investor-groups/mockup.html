What's new: a single new row in the existing kebab menu — "Investor mailing list…", between "Most Recent Application" and "Delete". When clicked it opens the panel below. If the company has no group, the panel opens with a primary "Create mailing list" button (Surface 3 prompt copy inline) instead of the member table. Re-sync re-runs the deployments → members diff (adds anyone missing; never removes). Delete uses a typed-confirm guard.

Behavior summary

Auto-provision: on the deployment that brings the company to 2+ unique investors. Single-investor companies don't get an auto-created list.
Auto-add: every subsequent deployment adds the new investor. Never auto-removes.
Exits: two paths — (1) member self-service via Google Groups +unsubscribe, or (2) admin removes them via the panel in Surface 4. The admin path is the primary one.
Anonymity: whoCanViewMembership=ALL_OWNERS_CAN_VIEW, replyTo=REPLY_TO_SENDER, includeInGlobalAddressList=false. Members see the address and the count, not the roster. (Caveat: when someone posts, recipients see the sender's From — anonymity covers the roster, not the messages.)
Slug: locked at creation. Renaming the company does not rename the group.
