Amanda White · Mar 24, 2026

Impact Investing Learning Lab — finding your place on the spectrum

Thanks to everyone who joined us for our Impact Investing Learning Lab cohosted with Bridgespan Social Impact! We covered the spectrum from finance-first to impact-first investing, explored the current climate investing landscape, and discussed how to evaluate the impact of investments.

One of the strengths of our community is that, as individual investors, where you fall on that spectrum is shaped by your own investment thesis. While many large climate funds sit at specific points along this range, each of you has the flexibility to define your own approach and decide where you want to invest.

The session generated excellent discussion about measurement frameworks, the difference between intentionality and additionality, and practical tools for assessing whether your investments are truly creating impact beyond financial returns.

Amanda White · Mar 10, 2026

Portfolio roundup: OpConnect closes $5M, TalkingHeads lands Vonu order, Volexion signs OEM talks

Happy to share some exciting portfolio company news across four companies:

OpConnect just closed $5M in debt financing and won't need to raise again until 2028 — a strong milestone for capital efficiency.

Lilac Solutions will deploy their ion-exchange lithium-extraction technology at PT Geo Dipa Energi's facilities in Indonesia, their first pilot in Southeast Asia.

Volexion has executed two JDAs with 6K Energy and Stratus Materials, and is in negotiations with several top-five global automotive OEMs.

TalkingHeads Wireless secured a ~$500K purchase order from Vonu, with expectations of materially larger follow-on orders and first field deployments in Ivory Coast and Nigeria by EOY 2026.
