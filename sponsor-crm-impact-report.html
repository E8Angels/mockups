Who supports our work

E8 Angels is a member-driven angel investing community accelerating climate-tech entrepreneurship. E8 Impact, our newly formed 501(c)(3), extends that work into education, fellowship, and ecosystem programs that are accessible to founders regardless of investor relationships.

Our supporters fund three programs: the Annual Fund (operations & member services, 501c6), the Fellowship Program (early-stage founders, 501c3), and Member Events (Summit and quarterly gatherings, both entities).

Two entities, one mission

E8 Angels · 501c6
Mission: deploy member capital to climate startups; build the community that surrounds them.
E8 Impact · 501c3
Mission: fund and run programs that strengthen the climate-tech ecosystem — independent of investor membership.
Gifts are routed to the entity that matches your tax preference and the program you're supporting.
