Acme Corp, a leader in AI-powered logistics optimization, today announced the successful close of a $5 million Series A funding round led by Venture Capital Partners, with participation from E8 Angels and other strategic investors.

The funding will be used to accelerate product development, expand the engineering team, and enter new markets. Since its founding in 2024, Acme Corp has grown revenue 300% year-over-year and now serves over 50 enterprise customers.

"We're thrilled to have the support of our investors," said Jane Smith, CEO of Acme Corp. "This funding will help us bring our AI logistics platform to the next level."

E8 Angels has been an early supporter of Acme Corp since its seed round in 2024. The company's innovative approach to logistics optimization using machine learning has positioned it as a category leader in the rapidly growing AI logistics space. Read more on the Acme Corp blog.
