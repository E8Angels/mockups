01

Introduce E8

E8 backs early-stage cleantech founders, typically raising a Seed round (Pre-Seed to Series A), building toward a thriving planet. If the company has no climate thesis, it isn’t a fit for E8, however strong it looks otherwise.

When someone fits, you’ll want a crisp description of E8 ready. This is the standard version.

Your 30-second introduction

E8 is a network of 150+ accredited impact investors backing early-stage cleantech companies since 2006. We’ve invested $75M+, typically $100K to $300K per company, at Pre-Seed to Series A. Our sweet spot is Seed. More at e8angels.com, and founders can apply at e8angels.com/entrepreneurs.

02

Understand the company

These are prompts, not a scorecard. You don’t need answers to all of them before making a referral. The goal is to spot a potentially interesting company and pass what you heard to the Screening Committee.

  • The challengeWhat specific problem are they solving, and how does their solution fit it?
  • ValidationWhat traction or proof do they have so far?
  • ScaleCould this become very large and deliver venture-scale returns?
  • Climate benefitWhat’s the climate or planetary benefit if they succeed?
  • Stage & timingAre they at Seed stage, and when does the round close?

Red flags

Any of these may mean the entrepreneur isn’t a good fit for E8.

  • No climate thesis, even when prompted
  • Founder comes across as arrogant
  • Uncapped, no-discount SAFE
  • Valuation that’s unrealistically high for the stage
03

Explain E8’s process

Set expectations. Founders mostly want to know what happens after they apply. This is the short version, written so you can paste it into an email as-is.

E8’s process, in brief

Here is how E8’s process works. There is no cost to apply, and you can apply any time at e8angels.com/entrepreneurs.

  1. Applications are reviewed at the start of each month.
  2. The top ~10 companies are invited to submit a video pitch of up to 3 minutes for the Screening Committee.
  3. The top three companies present to 50+ E8 members at the monthly meeting, in a 20-minute slot.
  4. If members show strong interest, we hold a follow-up call the following Monday or Tuesday and confirm that day whether we are moving to diligence.
  5. Diligence takes about four weeks.
  6. After diligence, the company is eligible for direct investment from E8 members and the E8 Annual Fund. Between the fund and direct investments from members, E8 typically invests between $200K - $500K in a round.

More about E8 at e8angels.com.

For anything beyond this, point them to the Entrepreneur FAQ ↗ or ask Sarah Bell (sarah@e8angels.com) rather than guessing.

If they’re not a good fit

  • Still submit concise, factual notes.
  • Select “Don’t Encourage.”
  • Don’t deliver an investment judgment for E8.

Follow-up email for a founder who fits

Copy it, then edit before sending. Keep the marked paragraph if you have advice from your conversation; delete it if you don’t.

Follow-up email

It was great to meet you.

To recap, E8 (e8angels.com) is a network of 150+ accredited impact investors backing early-stage cleantech companies. We’ve invested $75M+ since 2006, typically $100K – $300K per company, between Seed and Series A (our sweet spot is Seed stage). We invest in companies whose innovations increase the sustainability and health of our planet. There’s no cost to apply. Selected companies are given the opportunity to present (online) to our membership at one of our monthly meetings.

If you want to be considered to pitch to E8, apply on our website at e8angels.com/entrepreneurs. I’ve submitted my notes from our conversation to our Screening Committee. Once you submit:

  1. We review applications at the start of each month.
  2. The top ~10 companies selected through our Pre-Screening process have the opportunity to submit a video pitch of up to 3 minutes for consideration by our Screening Committee.
  3. The top three companies will present to 50+ E8 members later that month, in one of three 20-minute pitch slots between 10:00am and 12:30pm PT. (You’ll receive your exact slot time when invited to pitch.)
  4. If members show strong interest — for example, several want to explore investing further — we’ll invite you to a Follow-Up Call the following Monday or Tuesday, and confirm that day whether we’re moving forward to diligence.
  5. Diligence runs ~4 weeks from that date.
  6. Once diligence concludes (typically within four weeks of your pitch), you’re eligible for direct investment from our members and from the E8 Annual Fund. In 2025, the average investment was $153K per company.
Optional

Based on our conversation, there are a few things to consider before applying.

If you decide to apply, please let me know.

Best regards,
