All of these are already stored — no new capture. The structured deal terms live in instruments.metadata_json for the application; the raise figures live on the application row.

FieldSourceExampleWorth showing?
Min check sizedeal terms min_check_size_dollars$25,000Yes — asked for. Nearly always set (wizard defaults it).
Raised to datefunding_to_date_amount_cents$13,000,000Yes — asked for.
Non-dilutive to datenon_dilutive_amount_cents$4,744,000Yes — asked for. Reads best right beside Raised to date.
Valuation / capdeal terms valuation_dollars or cap_amount_dollars + pre/post$3,500,000 pre-moneyStrongest addition beyond your three. It is the number an angel checks first and it is currently only in prose.
Discountdeal terms discount_pct20%Yes, for SAFE / Note. Pairs with the cap; meaningless for equity.
Price per share · share type · liquidation preferencedeal terms$4.375 · Preferred · 1× non-participatingEquity rounds only. Preference is the one that matters at a $25k check; price per share rarely is.
Interest rate · maturitydeal terms6% · 24 monthsNotes only. Same slot as discount — instrument decides which appear.
Lead commitmentdeal terms lead_committed_cents$1,200,000Good — but as a suffix on the existing Lead Investor stat, not its own row.
Target close datedeal terms target_close_dateMay 29, 2026Careful — this is a second, different date from the Round Close stat (from the Round Close event). Enzinc has both, nine days apart. Either reconcile them or only show one.
Raise min – maxdeal terms raise_min_cents / raise_max_cents$2.0M – $3.0MReplace Capital Seeking with it where present. Capital Seeking is a free-text bucket ("1,500,000 or more"); this is the real number.
E8 interest registeredinvestment_interest sum$150,000 from 6 membersPossible, and E8-specific in a way none of the others are — but it nudges herd behaviour and needs a permission call. Ask before building.
Employeesnum_employees14No — company context, not deal terms. Belongs in Overview.
Note the overlap: the generated description already states the valuation, the close date and the lead in prose.
