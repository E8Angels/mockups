Sector & Tags

"Category" becomes Sector everywhere. The old secondary categories become Tags, in four families: Technology (a tree), Market, Business type and Enabling tech. Companies show one Sector pill and one Tags pill. The pill opens the full list, grouped by family.

1 · Company page and application flyout header

The Sector pill replaces today's category pill. The Tags pill shows a count and opens the list. Clicking any tag, or any level of a Technology path, opens Explore Companies filtered to that tag.

2 · Grids and lists

Admin grids, Explore Companies, Stage Review and Pitch History get a Sector column and a Tags column. Grid cells stay on one line: the most specific Technology tag is shown, then a count button that opens the same popover.

3 · Filtering: the tag picker

This one picker is used by every filter and every tag editor. With no search it browses the tree, grouped by family. Typing searches every level at once and shows each match with its path. Picking a parent includes everything under it, so choosing Batteries also finds companies tagged only Silicon anodes. Try typing "anode", "grid" or "hydrogen".

Matching rules
  • Search matches any word at any level, plus each tag's synonyms (e.g. "EV" → Electric vehicles).
  • Results show the full path, so "Anodes" under batteries and "Anodes" anywhere else can't be confused.
  • Selecting a parent selects its whole branch. Children appear checked and grey; unchecking the parent clears them.
  • Selected tags appear as removable chips at the top of the picker and on the filter button.
  • Keyboard: arrows move, Enter toggles, Escape closes.

4 · Editing Sector and Tags

Admins can change a company's Sector and Tags from the header's edit mode. AI tags are marked. A manual change is kept when the company is re-tagged later.
