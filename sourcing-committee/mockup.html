Front door vs side door

inbound apps vs vetted referrals

The general inbound application flow vs referrals from vetted sources (VCs, accelerators, labs, members).

Side-door deals convert ~4–5× better. Referred companies reach a pitch at 33% vs 7% for untagged inbound, and convert to investment at 12% vs 2.7%. Caveat: attribution is partial — some "front door" companies were in fact referred but never tagged, which overstates the gap. Another reason to fix capture.

Partner network strength

quality, not just quantity

How good a partner's dealflow is: deals referred → reached a pitch → dollars invested → exits → companies now closed. Click any partner for its referred-company list.

PartnerChannelRelationship Since Deals→ Pitch InvestedExitsClosed Effort h/yr
Quality ≠ volume. Greentown Labs sent the most deals (17) but only 1 reached a pitch and none are funded. Anthropocene Ventures sent 2, both pitched, drove $295K invested — yet both funded companies have since closed.
Read with care: n<5 flags partners with fewer than 5 referrals — too few deals to judge quality either way. Since matters too: older partners have had years to accumulate pitches, exits and shutdowns, so a 2025 partner with zero outcomes isn't underperforming — it's early. Effort h/yr is illustrative until owner time is captured in the portal.

Interest is a real but imperfect predictor. Emerald Battery Labs drew 27 interested members, 21 committed and 20 actually invested ($395K direct + $150K fund). But high-interest deals like It's Electric, Aslan Renewables and agapyo drew 22–27 interested members and 0 investors so far. The E8 Fund (56 voters in 2026) is a second, independent demand signal.
