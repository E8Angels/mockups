Mockup screen 14

Survey was changed — ask whether this is a new yearly cycle

Triggers automatically when admin picks a different survey on a survey-based rule. Plain language only — no jargon, no "cadence", no "state".

(Edit-rule screen behind the dialog — Survey field just changed from "E8 Annual Survey 2026" to "E8 Annual Survey 2027")

Is this a new yearly cycle?

You picked a different survey: E8 Annual Survey 2027.

If this is a new yearly cycle, we should forget who has already been reminded for the old survey. That way everyone who hasn't answered the new survey will be reminded fresh — even people who were reminded last year.

Two choices:
  • Start fresh — best for a new yearly survey. Everyone who hasn't responded to E8 Annual Survey 2027 will get reminders.
  • Keep history — best if you're just fixing a typo or you picked the wrong survey by accident. We won't re-remind people who were already reminded.
