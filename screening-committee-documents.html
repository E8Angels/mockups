Sync Model
Lazy sync from Google Drive: When the documents tile loads, we check the committee's Drive folder for changes (new files, renames, deletes). If anything changed, we update our index. This means users can work directly in Google Drive (create, rename, move files) and it'll be reflected next time the tile renders.

Our interface adds: pinning (metadata we store), our folder structure (which maps to Drive subfolders), and the presentation layer. "Remove from library" doesn't delete from Drive — it just hides the file from our interface. The Drive remains the source of truth for file content; we're the organizational/presentation layer.
