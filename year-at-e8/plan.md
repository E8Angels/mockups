# Year at E8 — Member Subway Map

## Background

Members need an at-a-glance view of what happens across the year at E8. This mockup uses a subway-style visual — four horizontal "lines" (Regular Programming, Annual Fund, Decarbon8, Events) running across twelve months — with a rich pop-out card for every month showing what to expect, real dates, and clickable actions.

## Proposed UX

- Four swim lanes: Regular Programming (top), Annual Fund, Decarbon8, Events (bottom).
- Twelve month tabs across the top, all white; click any tab to open that month's detail card.
- Stations on each lane mark signature events (Season begins, D8 Winners, SF/PNW/NY Climate Weeks, etc.).
- Each month's pop-out shows: real 2026 calendar dates for the standard rhythm (Screening Committee, Member Meeting, Follow-Up Calls, Diligence commitment, Fund vote commitment); month-specific highlights per lane; and CTAs (Join Member Meeting, Join Follow Up Call, Add Investment Interest).
- Placeholder portal URLs used for the CTAs and small-group link — swap for real URLs when known.

## Open questions

- Real portal URLs for the four CTAs (Member Meeting join, Follow-Up Call join, Investment Interest form, Small-group sign-up).
- Whether the Diligence team recap ("4–8 hrs / week") and Fund Member commitment ("~25 votes / year") reflect the current expectation the team wants published, or if we should refine.
- Whether the Events lane should carry more than the three Climate Weeks (e.g., Portfolio Showcase, Seattle Tech Week).
