Design Notes

Section 1 — Dashboard Tile

  • Replaces existing UpcomingEventsTile, removes hardcoded STATIC_EVENTS
  • Keeps timeline design (vertical line + colored dots per meeting type)
  • Date format: Wed, Apr 30 (day-of-week + month + day)
  • RSVP simplified: "Attending" badge (green) or nothing. No pending/declined/added states shown.
  • "View All →" in header links to /calendar
  • Max 5 events, sorted by date

Section 2 — Table View

  • Continuous scrollable list — sorted chronologically, past meetings at top (muted text), upcoming below. Auto-scrolls to first upcoming meeting on page load.
  • User can scroll up to see past meetings (with recording links). No separate "Past" section header.
  • Date format: Wed, Apr 30. Time: 11:30 AM. Both white-space: nowrap
  • Type pills: white-space: nowrap, short labels (Member, DD Debrief, Learning Lab, etc.)
  • No RSVP column — just a small "Attending" badge or empty
  • Unlabeled column for Zoom / Recording links — future meetings show "Join Zoom", past meetings show "Recording" (links to /meeting/:slug)
  • Add to Calendar button: blue tint background, shows Google/Apple/Outlook mini icons inline. Hover opens dropdown with provider options.
  • Already added: "✓ On your calendar" in muted text
  • Past rows: muted text color (#94a3b8), type pills at reduced opacity
  • Sticky header so columns stay labeled while scrolling

Section 3 — Calendar View

  • Stacked weekends (from PipelineMgtCalendarIsland pattern)
  • Color-coded event tiles by meeting type
  • Click tile → popover detail (Section 4)

Sections 6-7 — Mobile

  • Dashboard tile: same timeline, Wed, Apr 30 dates, "Attending" or nothing
  • Calendar page: card list only (no calendar grid on mobile)
  • Date block on left with type color bar
